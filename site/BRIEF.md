# ContaVias: build brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The owner asked for a
modern, clear site that shows exactly how the technology works, with a wow
factor, and asked me to start building so they can react to a real page. No
interview answers beyond that; everything below is an authored decision.

Evidence (from contavias.com.br, reviewed 2026-10-12): AI vehicle counting from
traffic video, results in about 48 h, sold by intersection type (single road,
T, X, plus quoted complex cases). Audiences: urban planners, traffic data
teams, traffic agencies, transport companies. Brazilian market, pt-BR first,
English added. Corporate proxy blocked the site afterwards, so no assets were
downloaded yet; everything visual is generated in code.

## The eight topics
1. **Vibe:** control room, daylight clarity, instrument-grade. Cold off-black,
   one orange accent (taken from the logo slashes). Refs: an air traffic radar
   scope, a Dieter Rams calculator, a Bloomberg terminal with the clutter removed.
2. **Journey:** see it working, understand each step, see what you receive,
   know what it costs, know who it is for, ask the last questions, start.
3. **Energy curve:** loud at the open (a running detector), calm and plain in
   the middle, one spike at the report, quiet resolve at the close.
4. **Feeling:** curiosity, then "oh, that is exactly what it does", then
   confidence, then ease. **Peak:** the junction hero where scroll peels the
   analysis on layer by layer.
5. **Never seen:** the page is the product. A real counter runs on a
   labelled-demo junction and the visitor steps through detect, classify,
   track, count, report with the wheel.
6. **Range:** modern, strict, instrument-like. Not playful.
7. **World:** one working surface (live surface grammar), not distinct scenes.
8. **Assets:** none yet. Logo and footage to be pulled from the live site once
   reachable. Nothing is generated or invented.

## Grammar
Live surface. Others lost: filmic needs footage we do not have and hides the
mechanism; chaptered editorial explains instead of showing; split stage only
serves the comparison, which is one section here; gallery has no objects.
Compromise: the hero carries a plain h1 and one CTA inside the surface panel,
because clarity for a first-time visitor outranks the grammar's no-headline rule.

## Honesty rules
- The junction is a labelled **demo simulation**. Counts, classes, report and
  flow diagram are computed from it. No fabricated customer data.
- No accuracy or time claims beyond the site's own (95%+, 48 h) with its own
  footnote carried over.
- Prices and WhatsApp number are from the current site.

## Feeling curve
| Act | Feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Console (pin) | Curiosity to clarity | Boxes appear on moving traffic; each scroll step adds one layer |
| Compare | Recognition | Their own manual process, in a plain table |
| Report | Confidence (peak of proof) | Real chart, flow diagram and CSV computed from the demo |
| Pricing | Control | Choosing an intersection redraws the movement map |
| Audience | Belonging | Four named roles |
| FAQ | Ease | Short direct answers |
| Close | Resolve | An input they can fill, one action |

**Peak:** "I scrolled and the traffic got boxed, labelled, tracked and counted
by movement." Lives in the console act.

**Tell-someone sentence:** it's the site where you watch the counter work
before you buy it.

## Authored silence
None. The hero act is long because it has five steps; every step changes state.
